Before we send anyone

What we check

  • NVQ Level 3 in electrotechnical services, plus the AM2 assessment for qualified status
  • 18th Edition wiring regulations, BS 7671, and whether the current amendment is held
  • ECS or JIB card grade and expiry, checked against the ECS database rather than a photo
  • 2391 inspection and testing where the role involves EICRs or certification
  • CSCS or ECS site card for access, plus any CompEx, IPAF or PASMA tickets the site asks for
  • Right to work documents and references from the last two employers
